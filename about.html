About Seed to Garden

We are an independent gardening website helping people across the United States grow seeds, plants, and produce with confidence.

Our story

Seed to Garden began with a simple idea: that growing your own plants should be doable, understandable, and enjoyable for everyone. We write clear, practical guides that meet beginners where they are — whether you have a backyard, a balcony, or just a sunny windowsill.

We focus on plants and produce that grow well across the varied climates of the United States. We explain basics like soil, sunlight, watering, and timing so you can learn by doing, and we keep the language friendly and jargon-free.

What we stand for

  • Honest information. We aim to be accurate, and we clearly flag when something is general guidance that may vary by region.
  • No false claims. We do not promise specific results, income, yields, or health outcomes. Gardening outcomes vary.
  • Beginner-friendly. We break things into small, achievable steps you can actually follow.
  • Transparent monetisation. If we use affiliate links, we say so clearly and never let them change our recommendations.

Not professional advice

Our content is for general informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional gardening advice, agricultural guidance, medical advice, or legal advice. For anything specific — such as fruit-tree rules in your state, soil testing, or health concerns — please consult a qualified professional.

If you would like to get in touch, please visit our contact page.
