Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Seed to Garden ("we", "our", "this website") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. If you contact us by email or our contact form, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we may gather limited technical data automatically, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This helps us understand how the site is used.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use cookies — small files stored on your device — and similar technologies. These help the site work correctly, remember preferences, and understand anonymous usage patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as intended.

3. Advertising and Google

We may display advertising on this website, including ads served by third-party advertising networks such as Google. These third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies (including the Google advertising cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to collect anonymous information about how visitors use the site, such as how long they stay and which pages are most visited. This data helps us improve our content. These tools may use cookies to collect this information.

5. How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and messages.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • To understand general visitor behaviour and preferences.
  • To display relevant content and advertising.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited, aggregated, or anonymous information with service providers (such as analytics or advertising partners) to operate the site. We may also disclose information when required by law.

7. Third-party links

Our website may link to external websites, such as retailers or educational resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their policies before providing any information.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can control cookies through your browser settings and manage advertising choices via the links above.

10. Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Email messages are typically retained only as long as necessary to respond and manage our correspondence.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of the site after changes takes effect means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at hello@seedtogarden.com or through our contact page.
